About FEDS

Designers who build hardware for India’s defence.

FuturEarth Design Solutions is a design-led deep-tech company. We build the sensors and the intelligence layer that help India’s armed forces see more and decide faster.

Our journey

From a design school
to a palace in London.

  1. NID AhmedabadTwo brothers, two design degreesIndustrial design, embedded systems, information design and enterprise UX between us.
  2. WildWatchOur first sensor systemA wildlife-vehicle collision avoidance system for forest roads. Our first step in building sensor systems for the field.
  3. December 2024Terra Carta Design Lab global winnerOne of eight winners worldwide in the competition founded by King Charles III and Sir Jony Ive. £100,000 to take WildWatch further.
  4. March 2025Hampton Court Palace, LondonWe represented India at the winners’ ceremony and met His Majesty King Charles III.
  5. 2026Into defenceFEDS is incorporated. Two defence nodes built and taken to Army officers in the field, who confirmed the need.
  6. NowOneViewJoining every sensor into one shared picture.
