Sense

Every platform reports in.

Vehicles, the ground post and the drones each send what they see, where they are and which way they are looking.

Check

A vehicle on the road.

OneView checks radar, sight, thermal and radio together. It marks the truck civilian and raises no alarm.

Task

A contact in the air.

OneView turns its own drone to look. Sight and thermal confirm a quadcopter, and the column is warned early.

Locate

Tanks behind the ridge.

The radar loses them, OneView keeps the track. A drone goes over the ridge and OneView pinpoints the tanks.

Each threat arrives with how sure OneView is and what it recommends. The decision stays with the commander.
